About Digital Saathi

Problem

Many people, especially beginners and less-experienced internet users, face difficulties using digital services safely and confidently.

Solution

Digital Saathi provides simple educational content, practical scenarios, quizzes, and safety guidance to improve digital literacy.

Objectives

  • Improve digital awareness
  • Teach safe online practices
  • Explain digital payments
  • Help users identify scams
  • Promote responsible internet usage
  • Increase confidence in using digital services
