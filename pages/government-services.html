🇮🇳 Government Digital Services

LEARN → PRACTICE → QUIZ → SCORE → PROGRESS

Simple Explanation

The government offers several official digital platforms to access documents and services safely — it's important to know how to identify the real ones.

Practical Examples

  • DigiLocker lets you store and access official documents like licenses and certificates digitally.
  • UMANG and MyGov are official apps for accessing various government services in one place.
  • Genuine government websites usually end in .gov.in or .nic.in.

Safety Tips

  • Always use the official government website or app.
  • Never click SMS links promising instant refunds.
  • Check that a website ends in .gov.in before entering personal details.
  • Never pay 'agents' extra money for guaranteed scheme approval.
  • Verify news about government schemes through official press releases.
