🔐 Online Safety

LEARN → PRACTICE → QUIZ → SCORE → PROGRESS

Simple Explanation

Staying safe online means protecting your passwords, personal information, and being alert to common tricks scammers use.

Practical Examples

  • A strong, unique password for each account makes it much harder for hackers to break in.
  • Two-factor authentication adds an extra step, like a code sent to your phone, for extra security.
  • Phishing messages look official but are designed to steal your information — always double-check before clicking.

Safety Tips

  • Use a unique, strong password for each account.
  • Turn on two-step verification wherever available.
  • Never share your OTP or password with anyone.
  • Don't click links from unknown senders.
  • Review your social media privacy settings regularly.
