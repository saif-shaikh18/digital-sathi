📱 Smartphone Basics

LEARN → PRACTICE → QUIZ → SCORE → PROGRESS

Simple Explanation

Your smartphone is a powerful tool once you know the basics — settings, connectivity, and how to keep it running smoothly and securely.

Practical Examples

  • Adjusting Wi-Fi and mobile data helps you stay connected and control how much data you use.
  • Installing and updating apps only from official stores like Google Play keeps your phone safe.
  • Managing storage and using screen lock protects your data and keeps your phone running well.

Safety Tips

  • Set a screen lock (PIN, pattern, or fingerprint).
  • Only install apps from Google Play Store or Apple App Store.
  • Keep your phone's software updated regularly.
  • Back up your photos and contacts to the cloud.
  • Turn off Bluetooth and Wi-Fi when not in use to save battery and reduce risk.
